## What I actually do

I'm a hands-on technical founder working across the part of software development where **product decisions and engineering decisions meet**.

I work with founders on products that are being:

* taken from **idea → MVP → launch**
* turned into real **SaaS businesses**
* built across **web and mobile**
* monetized through **subscriptions and payments**
* structured around **marketplaces, bookings and workflows**
* inherited from previous developers
* rescued from unstable or unfinished builds
* extended after launch without destroying what already works

I care about writing good software.

I care even more about making sure we're building the **right software in the first place**.

---

## The work behind the code

A lot of software problems don't begin with code.

They begin earlier:

```text
What should version one actually contain?

Should this feature exist yet?

Does this need custom engineering or would a simpler system work?

What happens when payments fail?

What happens when real users behave differently than expected?

Should this part be fixed, refactored or rebuilt?

Will today's shortcut become next year's bottleneck?
```

Those are the questions I like working on.

Then I build.

---

## How I think about products

> **Version one should prove the business deserves version two.**

> **Technology should follow the product — not the other way around.**

> **Every feature has two costs: building it today and maintaining it tomorrow.**

> **A product can look almost finished and still be far from ready.**

> **Progress isn't measured only by software written. It's also measured by uncertainty removed.**

## Founder × Product × Engineering

I don't see these as separate worlds.

```text
Founder intent
      ↓
Product decision
      ↓
Technical architecture
      ↓
Implementation
      ↓
Real users
      ↓
Evidence
      ↓
Next product decision
```

That's the loop.

Good engineering should make the next business decision easier — not just add more code.
